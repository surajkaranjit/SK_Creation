Behind the Lens

Hello! I am Suraj Karanjit, the creative energy behind SURAJ CREATION. Based in Kathmandu, my practice lives at the intersection of moving video, narrative continuity, and raw high-contrast photography.

By blending my technical background in video editing and cinematic color grading with a keen eye for still frames, I capture stories with deliberate structure. I focus heavily on sculpting shadow metrics, natural lighting balances, and emotional pacing blocks.
